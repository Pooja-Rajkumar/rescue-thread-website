🐾 The problem

Critical information lives everywhere.

Rescue teams often track key information across shelter software, spreadsheets, forms, Slack, and email. Reconstructing a dog's full history can be time-consuming and create bottlenecks for staff and volunteers.

🗂️
Shelter systems

The solution

A unified timeline for every dog.

Rescue Thread organizes care and behavior events into a per-dog timeline, with filters that help teams find relevant history.

The product

Care updates and searchable records.

Capture updates as they happen

Add a care or behavior observation with context such as when, where, and what happened.

Find information across dogs

Search and filter recorded events by dog, behavior concern, and date.

Built with Muttville

From a real rescue, for rescue teams.

Rescue Thread began through work with Muttville Senior Dog Rescue in San Francisco, where care and behavior information was spread across different tools.
